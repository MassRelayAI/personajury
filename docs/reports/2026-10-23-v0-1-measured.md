# PersonaJury v0.1 — measured

## What was built
One binary `personajury` (Rust 1.98.1, Apache-2.0), SQLite at `<root>/.personajury/store.db`, an MCP server with five tools
(`personajury_brief`, `_verdict`, `_matrix`, `_delta`, `_should_run`; no tool can ask for a cross-strata average), two skills,
the four shipped lenses as data, governance and docs in the shape of the 175 surveyed OSS repositories. Verbs: persona
add|generate|list, panel add|resolve, lens add|list, subject add, comparator add, brief, verdict, matrix, pick, delta,
check, decide (D4), should-run, setup, doctor, mcp. The engine has no HTTP client in its tree (`deny.toml` bans them;
`tests/guards.rs` checks `Cargo.lock`) and reads no env var named like a key or token.

## The final review (fresh Opus reader, then one fix wave and one scoped re-review)
The whole-engine review found 3 Critical and 9 Important defects, all reproduced, in the gates built on the refusals:
a fix decided `fixed` and raised again passed `check`; `delta` said `landed` with verdicts missing; re-ingesting without a
blocker cleared it; delta added blockers across strata; a new subject version left old runs fresh; `matrix` merged two
subjects; a `recalled` score read like a `seen` one; any later run cleared `fixed_unverified`; a persona id could write a
brief outside the workspace; `license` or `Money` slipped past the human classes; git-quoted paths vanished from
`should-run`; `about` was outside the finding id. One fix wave closed all twelve (re-review: 12/12 addressed, each
reproduction re-run old → new, no new Critical or Important); the recorded verdicts and briefs were not touched.
Reviews: `docs/reports/2026-10-23-v0-1-final-review.md`, `…-final-rereview.md`.

### What the acceptance run caught (three rounds, all evidence kept in the maintainer's notes)
1. **Round 0:** `about: cmp:acme-pricing` carried two meanings (Ana and Claire scored Acme itself 5; Mei and George
   scored Lantern against Acme 2 and 1) — the brief's example put the comparator in `about` on every line. Fixed: `about`
   names the option scored, on its own; the subject against the comparators leaves `about` out (commit 852da52).
2. **Round 1:** jurors scored every attachment as an option even under `critical` (35 columns, the walk and the PNG of
   one page scored twice). Fixed: `about` exists only under a lens that picks or compares (45e9b19); the compare briefs
   stayed byte-identical, so only the critical jurors re-ran.
3. **Juror independence:** two of seven round-1 jurors disclosed globbing the shared verdict folder; they and one more
   with the same tool-call signature were re-run in private folders; the skill now says so. The recorded run is
   the isolated critical round plus the clean compare round.

## Open — the maintainer answers (not guessed)
1. **The held-out detector's target.** The design names a hand-scored jury table the maintainer keeps privately, but the round it cites
   is four personas picking a hostname, with no dimensions; the six criteria A–F are in another table (a 1–10 scale).
   Fable recommends the second round of that table, ranks per stratum, within one rank on the primary stratum, with a 1–10 lens; that lens
   ships as data at `fixtures/lenses/site.toml` (anchors 1/4/7/10, loaded with `lens add`, not seeded). Which table, and
   is that scale right?
2. **What an option is under `compare` (and for `pick`).** Today: each subject attachment label and `cmp:<name>`. The run
   shows the cost: 18 lines per juror, the walk and screenshot of one page as two options, and "pricing.png" picked to mean
   "our site". Candidate: the subject as one option plus each comparator, with attachments as options only for kinds whose
   files are alternatives (logo, design). Which?
3. **What a "split" is (report §10 escalation).** Not built: the engine reports per stratum and primary blockers but makes
   no escalate call. Fable recommends "on some (lens, dimension), two strata whose `anchor_met` ranges do not overlap, or
   any primary-stratum blocker". Confirm before the engine flags escalation.
(Also for the maintainer's eye, already decided provisionally: D4 `decide` as the writer of decisions.)

## Deferred minors (for v0.2)
- `should-run` reads `--- ` lines inside a hunk as headers (fails closed: `invalid_diff` or a too-high quorum);
- `check` orders a fix and its re-raise by run id, not ingest time (a point raised again by a run briefed before the
  decision shows as `fixed_unverified`, not `fix_not_landed` — visible, but labelled softer);
- the "same point" key of report §10(3) has two known ways around it (rewording the citations); provisional (D5, D6);
- the human-class guard trusts the class labels a project writes: plurals and synonyms (`secrets`, `pricing`, `billing`)
  under a persona quorum pass — a path-based floor the engine owns (e.g. `**/*.env*`, `LICENSE*`) is the candidate;
- walk link targets live in node names (`Sign up free (#)`), no `href` field; no `brief#<section>` locator for `told` lines.

2026-10-24 follow-up: open question 3 (what counts as a split) is closed by D13 (`docs/decisions.md`): two strata whose `anchor_met` ranges on a cell do not overlap. `matrix` reports `splits`; `apply-check` uses them.

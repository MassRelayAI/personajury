## What was measured about the graph

- Same store, same bytes: tested twice and on the committed asset (1200 x 902, from the recorded fixture run).
- A single persona with zero findings, a run with no verdict and 100 personas all render as well-formed XML (review probes).
- Height grows with the sum of the strata, not the tallest one (the columns descend like stairs, D11): 100 personas give a card 5302 px tall. After the review this was changed: above 30 personas the strata sit side by side and the height follows the tallest column.

## D12 hash change

are byte-identical and `fixture_replay` is green.

## What the independent review found

The reviewer re-ran the suite (325 passed), then probed a clean copy with 12 extra tests. No Critical.

| id | finding | how it was fixed |
|---|---|---|
| Important 1 | a reported usage above `i64::MAX` was accepted, stored wrapped, then crashed `matrix` and `doctor` (debug) or printed a wrong total (release) | such a value is `invalid_usage` at the parse; sums saturate |
| Important 2 | after a second `fixed` decision the history lost the `fix_not_landed` step | a finding's life uses every `fixed` decision; raised, fixed, raised again, fixed again shows all four steps |
| Important 3 | a malformed `jurypersona.toml` was ignored silently by `matrix` and `doctor`, with a default estimate | `matrix` refuses it (`invalid_config`); `doctor` answers and lists it as a limitation item |

Minor, all fixed: M1 XML-invalid characters (U+FFFE, U+FFFF, C0 controls) dropped from SVG text; M2 with many rounds the dots left the finding box,
so the strip draws the last 8 runs with a `+N` mark; M3 an empty `--comparator` element is `invalid_arguments`; M4 height (above); M5 a separate
variable (`JURYPERSONA_ASSET_REGEN`) regenerates the graph asset, apart from the replay's.

Verified as asked: a brief above 12 seats is refused without a reason on every path (CLI query, saved panel, `--spend 13`, lens lists, MCP); 100 seats
give waves of 30/30/30/10 in brief order; decision colours and `<desc>` counts match the store.

## What is left open

- Finding labels show about 20 characters at width 1200; the full text is the tooltip.
- Scores and medians over several rounds come from the latest run of each lens (stated in D11).

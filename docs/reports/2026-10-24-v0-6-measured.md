# PersonaJury v0.6 — measured

v0.6 was built in five lanes. A: traits, cover and slices. B: traits proven. C: options, deliberation, the counted metrics and
decision juries. D: the walk exporter. E: export and the chat pack. A, B and C each had an independent review, then a fix round.
D and E had none. The numbers below come from each lane's own report and from the reviews; the release run is this report's own.

## What each review found, and how it was fixed

**Lane A: 0 Critical, 5 Important, 6 Minor.**

| id | finding | fix |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | `--seats auto` broke the effort caps: `high` was refused where `max` worked, and `low` seated 4 | the cap is the whole jury, the control inside it: 13 serving + 1 unrelated seats low 2+1, medium 6+1, high 11+1 |
| I2 | a protected attribute entered as a trait under a near-synonym id (`demo.age_band`) | a screen on every added trait; since D39 a hit is a label, not a refusal |
| I3 | the `words` trait copied free text into the brief | `trait_text_refused` (80 characters, no digit, no "You are"); rendered "Your work: ..." |
| I4 | `trait install` silently reverted a user's edited trait | origin `edited`, install keeps it (`kept_local`); `--force --reason` overwrites and records why |
| I5 | `--seats auto` could never reach `apply` | auto records no override by itself; a new reason `thin_primary` (fewer than 2 judged primary seats) advises |

**Lane B: 1 Critical, 4 Important, 9 Minor.**

| id | finding | fix |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | a category trait's options and a band's anchors were never screened | every text of a trait and a persona's prose is read; the maintainer then ruled "allow, label, record" (D39): a hit is a `sensitive` label, recorded on the run and shown in `summary` |
| I1 | a material named `access` turned off a stem in every generated id | the exemption is gone; the shipped catalog is labelled by hand |
| I2 | the stem screen missed compounds, other languages, look-alikes and split words | one table of 64 stems, a crate-free normalisation, a second read with separators removed |
| I3 | `suggest --check` took a one-letter quote, or one across two fields | at least 3 words and 12 characters inside one field (`quote_too_short`, `quote_not_found`) |
| I4 | the three-juror rule was untested at its edge | three tests: a stratum of two, a two-score cell, a superseded batch |

Of the Minor items, M1 (`label_echo` on traits the juror never saw) was fixed; M2 to M9 are open.

**Lane C: 4 Critical, 3 Important, 4 Minor.**

| id | finding | fix |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | an initial verdict was accepted after `deliberate`, so a deliberated run could reach `apply` | refused (`initial_closed`); `apply-check` advises once the round exists |
| C2 | the questions file had no strict grammar | only `## Q<n> [class: c] text`, `- option` and blank lines; any other line is `invalid_questions` naming it |
| C3 | a money or legal question could be declared a jury class | the class is required; a jury class whose text hits the stem table answers `human` (`class_mismatch`) |
| C4 | a split between strata could still answer `apply` | a split answers `human` on money, spending, trust, legal or public-claim, else `advise` |
| I1 | point-of-view sources were not scanned | listed as "Sources mention", information only |
| I2 | the starter config shipped the human rules off | they ship on, and the surface class at `high` |
| I3 | a v0.5 binary could open a v0.6 store | schema version 6; a v0.5 binary refuses it by its version |

M1, M2 and M4 were fixed; M3 (`phases.deliberated` repeats the shift objects) is left, because removing it breaks an equality a D33
test pins.

## What `trait observed` says on the fixture

Lane B ran `trait observed` over the 14 recorded fixture verdicts. Of the 53 traits the seven personas declare:

| word | count | which |
|---|---|---|
| consistent | 4 | builder-ana's decision style, price consciousness and marketing skepticism; builder-ravi's need for cognition |
| contradicted | 3 | newcomer-sofia's domain expertise 1 and rejection sensitivity 4; company-george's need for cognition 1 |
| no_signal | 46 | no rule in the observe map fires, or the stratum is too small to compare |

What it means:
- **Sofia's two are one observation.** Both come from the same clarity cells: she scored clarity at or above her peers, never below.
- **George's is an artefact.** The fixture records only `seen` lines, so "recalled over seen" can never show. A persona declared low on
  need for cognition cannot show it here.
- **Most traits are silent.** The secondary stratum holds two jurors and the bystander one. A comparison needs three jurors in a
  stratum (D35), so they give counts only.
- **No trait map is shown wrong.** Four primary jurors over two runs is too small. It is a bearing, not a measurement. It says how a
  persona was played, never how real people behave.

## What is left open

- **Brief size.** Eight traits add 23.9% to a compact brief and 15.5% to a full one, above the 15% the design set as a detector; five
  traits add 14.7%. Documented as a cost in D29, not trimmed.
- **A word that hits by chance.** newcomer-sofia studies public health, so the fixture summary now carries a "simulated demographics:
  health" line. A phrase exception or reading traits only is to decide.
- **Minor items open:** A M1 to M6; B M2 to M9; C M3. A shipped caveat (`social.stigma_consciousness`) still says
  "protected attribute"; changing it changes the trait's hash.
- **Walk ids.** The fixture's walk ids are hand-written, so they differ from the ids `tools/walk.mjs` generates. Re-cite from a captured
  walk. Lane D's capture tests skip without Chrome or Node 22.
- **Fixed before the release: `export` and a closed pipe.** `personajury export RUN | head` panicked with "failed printing to
  stdout: Broken pipe" once `head` closed. Found while writing `docs/own-metrics.md`; a test that closes the pipe first went
  red, and every CLI answer now goes through one writer that treats a closed pipe as a normal end (`tests/v06e_export_chat.rs`,
  `a_closed_pipe_ends_export_quietly`).
- **Not verified outside this machine:** the Action on GitHub, the `.mcpb` bundle installed by hand in Claude Desktop, and the chat pack
  in a real web chat.
- **Smaller gaps:** `--phase` is CLI-only on `delta`, `graph` and `calibrate`; there is no MCP `init`; the held-out check (`testing.md`)
  is still to report.

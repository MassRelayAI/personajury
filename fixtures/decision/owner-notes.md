# Owner notes (point of view)

The first five minutes decide whether a developer stays. A checklist that asks for an account before anything works loses
people at the door; one that lets them install and see a result first keeps them.

Prices are public words: once the Team price is on the pricing page, changing it costs trust. Small teams compare us with
tools at 9 and 12 dollars a seat, and the finance reader wants a price that needs no approval chain.

Money is tight this quarter. Every outside contract needs a reason a customer would recognise, and the security review is
something the two owners can do themselves if they take a week.
